About the Author

Kim Wild

Kim Wild crafts captivating children's tales inspired by her Montana GreenHouseSheShed, blending real photos with AI magic to enchant readers of all ages.

The GreenHouseSheShed Stories are gentle, magical tales that weave together kindness, friendship, belonging and wonder. Set in a charming garden world filled with fairies, Flutterbys, SweetBees, Fuzzy Buzzys, and woodland friends, each story encourages children to dream, explore and care for one another while sparking the imagination.

"Step inside with us… may the magic find you."

Where It All Began

The Real GreenHouseSheShed

Every story in this world is rooted in a real place, and real friends.

Thank You for Stepping Into the Magic With Us

Created with love and patience, the Magic Series of 5 books and the new Discover Adventure Series are based on my real GreenHouseSheShed — the real Miss Bluebeary, a bear cub who moved into our Douglas Fir tree and made her home beneath the branches, the real honeybees and bumblebees and flowers that live with us, our forever puppy Echo, the deer who live in our four-acre backyard, our little forest, and the little humans who help me.

Illustrated using my real photos with AI to bring on the magic. No AI was used in the writing.
